How event contracts fit alongside the options and stocks you already trade.

I'm the founder of Quantcha, where I've built options analytics and trading tools for active traders since 2014. More recently I've built the same kind of tooling for prediction markets and event contracts, and both now live in one platform. I write and speak about what event contracts mean for people who trade options and stocks, including the parts of the options playbook that don't carry over.

About

Ed Kaim is the founder of Quantcha, a financial software company specializing in equity options. Since 2014 he's built an end-to-end options trading platform and sold proprietary options analytics to market makers, quant funds, and hedge funds.

His current work sits where options, equities, and event contracts overlap. He has built analytics on each side, and his writing covers how prediction markets actually behave, what they add for an options or stock trader, and where the options playbook stops working. He publishes the Options & Prediction Markets newsletter on LinkedIn.
