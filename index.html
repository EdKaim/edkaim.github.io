About

Ed Kaim is the founder of Quantcha, a financial software company specializing in equity options. Since 2014 he's built an end-to-end options trading platform and sold proprietary options analytics to brokerages, hedge funds, and market makers.

His current work sits where options, equities, and event contracts overlap. He has built analytics on each side, and his writing covers how prediction markets actually behave, what they add for an options or stock trader, and where the options playbook stops working. He publishes the Options & Prediction Markets newsletter on LinkedIn.
